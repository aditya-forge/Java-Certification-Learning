# Asking for help

When I'm stuck and posting on a forum (or asking anyone), a good question gets a useful answer much faster.

What to include:

- What I'm trying to do, in one or two sentences
- The smallest piece of code that shows the problem, not the whole project
- What I expected to happen and what actually happened
- The exact error message, copied as text rather than a screenshot
- What I already tried

What to avoid:

- Titles like "Help!!" or "Code not working". Something like "NullPointerException when reading second file" is far more useful.
- Posting full solutions to graded assignments. Ask about the concept or the specific error instead.

Often, cutting the code down to the smallest failing example shows me the bug before I even post.
